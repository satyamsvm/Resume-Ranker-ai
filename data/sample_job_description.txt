Machine Learning Engineer

computer vision features. You will work closely with the product team.

- Hands-on NLP experience (spaCy, NLTK or Hugging Face transformers)
- Experience building REST APIs with Flask or FastAPI
- Docker and Git; familiarity with AWS is a plus
- Good communication and problem solving skills
